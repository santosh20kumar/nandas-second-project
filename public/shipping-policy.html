Delivery / Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Nanda's Creative provides local delivery in Hyderabad for orders placed through this website, subject to service-area availability.

Delivery charge

The standard delivery fee is ₹50. Delivery is free for orders above ₹299, subject to the applicable delivery area.

Delivery timing

We aim to prepare and deliver orders as promptly as possible. Cakes and other fresh products may require preparation time. Exact delivery timing can vary with order volume, traffic, weather and product availability.

Delivery address

Please provide a complete and accurate address, phone number and PIN code at checkout. If the delivery team cannot reach you because of incorrect details or the recipient is unavailable, delivery may be delayed or rescheduled.

Fresh products

Because bakery products are prepared fresh, please receive and store them appropriately after delivery. Special handling instructions should be included in the order notes where relevant.

Delivery issues

If your order arrives damaged, incorrect or substantially different from what you ordered, contact us as soon as possible with your order number and details so we can review the issue.
