Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

We prepare bakery products to order, so cancellation and refund eligibility can depend on whether preparation has started and on the nature of the product.

Order cancellation

Please contact Nanda's Creative as soon as possible if you need to cancel an order. Cancellation requests received before preparation may be considered for a full refund for prepaid orders.

After preparation has started

Once preparation or dispatch has started, cancellation may not be possible for fresh or customised products. We will review requests case by case.

Incorrect or damaged orders

If you receive an incorrect or materially damaged item, contact us promptly with your order number and details. Where appropriate, we may offer a replacement, refund or other reasonable resolution after reviewing the issue.

Refund processing

Approved refunds for online payments will be initiated through the payment method or payment gateway used for the transaction. The time for the amount to appear in your account can depend on the payment provider and bank.

Cash/UPI on delivery

For orders that have not been prepaid, there is no online payment amount to refund. Any adjustment will be discussed with the customer based on the specific order issue.

Contact

For cancellation or refund requests, contact us on WhatsApp at 9000000000 and mention your order number.
